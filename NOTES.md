The first release of MyHabits, a free habit tracker for Android.

- Tick, count, timed and quit habits, on any schedule
- Streaks with rest days, freezes and a holiday break
- Celebrations for milestones, and an animated opening
- 120 ready-made habits and twelve 30-day journeys
- Reminders with Done, Snooze and Rest day buttons
- Statistics, a page for every habit, levels and 38 badges
- Notes, and an optional daily mood check-in
- Three home-screen widgets
- Backup to a file or a folder, and export to a spreadsheet
- No adverts, no account, and no internet access

Needs Android 8 or later. Download `MyHabits.apk` below and open it to install.

Full notes: https://customgpter.github.io/myhabits/releases.html
