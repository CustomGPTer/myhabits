Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026. This policy covers the MyHabits app for Android (package uk.jj.myhabits) and this website. MyHabits is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom.

The short version

  • Your habits, your history, your notes, your mood and your settings are kept on your phone. They are never uploaded, to the developer or to anyone else.
  • MyHabits does not hold Android's internet permission. It cannot go online, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • MyHabits has no accounts and no servers. The developer receives nothing from your phone.
  • There is no advertising and no analytics in the app.
  • Copies leave the app only when you make them: a backup file, the backup folder, a spreadsheet or a shared picture. The detail is below.

What stays on your phone

MyHabits keeps everything in the app's private storage, in a small database that other apps cannot read:

  • Your habits: their names, icons, colours, goals, schedules, reminder times and the journey they belong to, if any.
  • Your history: for each habit and day, whether it was done, rested or missed, the amount or the minutes, and any note you wrote.
  • Your mood for a day and the line that goes with it, if you have switched the mood check-in on.
  • Your settings, the badges you have earned, your level, and any breaks you have set.
  • Which habit each One habit widget follows.
  • The folder you picked for automatic backups, if you picked one.
  • A copy of your data from just before the last time you restored a backup, so that a restore can be undone.

MyHabits does not ask for the internet, your location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, photos, phone or messages.

Services the app contacts

None. The app makes no connections, because it cannot. It does not check for updates, report crashes or load anything from the internet.

One thing outside the app is worth knowing. Android can back up app data to your Google account as part of its own device backup, and can copy it to a new phone during setup. If you have that switched on, MyHabits' data may be included. That backup is run by Google under your account and your phone's settings, and the developer has no access to it.

Notes, mood and health

Some of what you record can be personal: a habit such as stopping smoking or taking medication, a note about a bad day, or your mood. MyHabits treats all of it the same way. It stays on your phone and is used only to show you your own progress.

The mood check-in is off until you switch it on, and switching it off hides it without deleting what you logged.

Backups and exports

  • A backup file holds everything listed above except the phone-only details, such as the backup folder. It is plain text and is not encrypted: anyone with the file can read it.
  • The automatic backup saves the same kind of file into the folder you picked, once a day on days you use the app, and keeps the newest seven. If that folder belongs to a cloud storage app, that app will upload the files under its own terms.
  • A spreadsheet export holds your habits, every day logged with its notes, and your mood.
  • A shared picture holds only what you can see on it, such as a habit's name and its streak.

Where these files go after you save or share them is up to you, and is covered by the privacy policy of whatever app or service you send them to.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept until you remove it. You can:

  • Delete a habit in its editor, which removes its history and notes too.
  • Clear a single day by opening it and removing the tick or the note.
  • Remove everything by uninstalling the app, or with Clear storage in Android's settings for the app.
  • Delete backup files, spreadsheets and the files in the backup folder yourself, in your phone's Files app. Uninstalling the app does not remove them.

Your rights

UK and EU data protection law gives you rights over personal data that an organisation holds about you, including the right to see it, correct it and have it deleted.

The developer of MyHabits holds no personal data about you: there is no account, no server and no log of who uses the app. Your habits, history, notes and mood are on your own phone, where you control them directly using the steps above. If you need help, use the contact link below. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Security

The app's data is held in private storage that other apps cannot read. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app, as MyHabits has no lock of its own.

A backup file is checked before it is restored. MyHabits reads only what it expects to find, keeps values within sensible limits, shows you what the file holds before anything is replaced, and keeps a copy of what was there.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other websites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub, as the host, receives the internet address of visitors and handles it under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The app file is downloaded from GitHub as well, on the same terms.

Children

MyHabits is a general-purpose app and is not directed at children. Its developer does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
