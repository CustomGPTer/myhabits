What MyHabits is

MyHabits is a habit tracker: a place to record what you did and to be reminded and encouraged. It is not a medical device, a treatment, a coach, a therapist or a source of medical, dietary, psychological or financial advice.

The ready-made habits, the journeys, their goals and the daily lines of guidance are general examples. They are not tailored to your health, your fitness, your circumstances or any medication you take. Any habit you add or change is your own choice.

Your health

Speak to a doctor, pharmacist or other qualified professional before you change how much you exercise, what you eat or drink, how you sleep, or how you take any medication, particularly if you are pregnant, have a health condition, have or have had an eating disorder, or are under 18. Do not use MyHabits against medical advice.

Stop an activity and seek medical attention if you feel pain, dizziness, faintness, shortness of breath, chest discomfort or any other symptom that concerns you.

If you are struggling with your mood, or a habit has stopped feeling like a choice, please talk to your GP or call NHS 111. In an emergency call 999. Samaritans can be reached free on 116 123 at any time.

Health statements in the app

Some journeys carry a short line about health, such as that regular activity lowers your risk of heart disease, stroke, type 2 diabetes and some cancers, or that stopping smoking lowers your risk of cancer, heart disease and stroke. These lines summarise public guidance from the NHS and Cancer Research UK as the developer understood it when the app was written.

They describe risk across a population. They are not a promise about you, they do not say that any habit prevents or cures a disease, and they may fall out of date. For the current guidance, see nhs.uk/live-well and cancerresearchuk.org.

Giving something up

A quit habit counts days. It does not provide treatment or support. If you are stopping smoking, free NHS stop smoking services improve your chances. If you drink heavily or depend on alcohol or another drug, stopping suddenly can be dangerous: get medical advice first.

Streaks, statistics and mood

Streaks, rates, levels, badges and charts are worked out from what you tick, on your phone, using the rules described in the app. They are for encouragement and are not a measure of your health or your worth. The mood chart shows what you logged. Where it sets mood beside habits, it shows that two things happened together, not that one caused the other.

Reminders and widgets

Reminders depend on your phone's settings and battery saving, and can arrive late or not at all. Do not rely on MyHabits for anything where a missed or late reminder could cause harm, such as taking medication or keeping an appointment. Widgets can show an out-of-date picture for a short time.

Your data and backups

Your data is kept on your phone and is your responsibility. A phone can be lost, reset or broken. Save a backup file from time to time, or set up the automatic backup, if your history matters to you. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere you trust. Restoring a backup replaces what is on the phone.
