Habits, journeys and sounds

The ready-made habits, the twelve journeys and their daily lines were written for MyHabits by the developer. The health statements in the journeys summarise public guidance from the NHS and Cancer Research UK in the developer's own words. Neither organisation has reviewed or endorsed the app.

Every sound in MyHabits is generated from scratch as simple tones. No recordings or samples are used, so there is nothing to credit.
