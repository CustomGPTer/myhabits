# MyHabits

MyHabits is a free habit tracker for Android. Tick off what you did, watch the day's ring fill and keep your streaks going. It has tick, count, timed and quit habits, streaks with rest days and freezes, twelve 30-day journeys, reminders with buttons, statistics, badges, three home-screen widgets and backup. It has no adverts, no accounts and no paid version, and it cannot go online.
